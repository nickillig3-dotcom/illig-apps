Past Sprints, Sprint History & Scope Change Gadgets for Jira — last updated 24 August 2026

The short version: this app stores nothing. It runs entirely on Atlassian's own infrastructure, reads the Jira issues you already have access to in order to draw a number on your dashboard, and forgets them as soon as the gadget has rendered. No data is sent anywhere else, and there is no server of ours for it to be sent to.

What the app does with your data

The app is an Atlassian Forge app. Forge apps run on infrastructure operated by Atlassian, inside Atlassian's own cloud — this app qualifies for Atlassian's Runs on Atlassian programme, which means it uses no external hosting of any kind.

When a dashboard gadget is displayed, the app calls Jira's REST API on your behalf and reads:

  • issues matching the filter or project you configured in the gadget,
  • for each issue: its key, summary, current status, sprint field and the numeric field you chose to measure — only the fields needed for the figures shown,
  • the change history of those issues, restricted to entries for the Sprint and status fields. This is unavoidable: which issues were in a sprint when it started, and which were added afterwards, exists nowhere else in Jira,
  • the list of Jira field definitions and the list of statuses, in order to detect which custom fields hold sprints and estimates on your site and which statuses count as done,
  • the names of your projects and of your favourite filters, so the gadget settings can offer them.

Those values are used to compute the totals and then discarded. They are held only in memory for the duration of the request that renders the gadget.

What the app does not do

  • No storage. The app writes nothing to Forge storage, no database, no cache, no logs of your issue data.
  • No transmission to third parties. There is no external endpoint, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, no tracking pixels.
  • No profiling and no automated decision-making.
  • No access beyond your own. The app calls Jira as the viewing user, so it can only ever read issues that user is already permitted to see. It cannot widen anyone's access.

Permissions the app requests

read:jira-work — read issues, fields and projects. This is the only scope requested, and it is read-only. The app never creates, edits or deletes anything in your Jira.

Personal data under the GDPR

Issue data may incidentally contain personal data — an assignee's display name, for example. The app processes such data only transiently to render a total, on the instruction of your organisation as the controller, and never retains it. Atlassian remains the processor for the underlying Jira data; the legal basis for that processing is your organisation's agreement with Atlassian. Because nothing is retained, there is no data of yours held by us to export, correct or erase.

Sub-processors

None. The app has no infrastructure outside Atlassian's.

Data location

Wherever your Atlassian site is hosted. The app introduces no additional location, and no data crosses a border that Atlassian's own hosting does not already cross.

Security incidents

Since no data is stored, a compromise of the app could not expose retained customer data. Should a security issue nonetheless arise, it will be reported to affected customers and to Atlassian without undue delay via the contact address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to store data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes on the Atlassian Marketplace.
