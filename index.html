Atlassian Marketplace apps by Nick Illig

Story Points Sum & Epic Progress

Two Jira Cloud dashboard gadgets: sum story point estimates across a project, filter or JQL query grouped by epic, assignee, status, sprint or label — and track completion progress for an epic. Built on Atlassian Forge; runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure and stores nothing.

Past Sprints, Sprint History & Scope Change

Two Jira Cloud dashboard gadgets: every past and current sprint in one list — committed, added after the start, removed, delivered and carried over — and the same breakdown for a single sprint down to the issue. Blocked Issues, Dependency & Linked Issues

Two Jira Cloud dashboard gadgets: every work item waiting on an unfinished blocker — who holds it, in what state, and how many days it has been stuck — and a matrix of which projects or epics block which, with the cross-project pairs marked. Built on Atlassian Forge; runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure and stores nothing.
